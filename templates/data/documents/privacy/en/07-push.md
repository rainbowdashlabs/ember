## Push notifications

Push notifications are optional. They are switched on only once you expressly allow them on a device, and they can be removed at any time: in the notification settings, in the list of your sign-ins or in the browser's settings.

- For each device you allow push notifications on, we store the delivery address the browser issues, two keys for encryption and a short name of the device. The keys and the name belong to the sign-in in which you allowed them and are deleted with it. We keep the delivery address together with whether you allowed push on the device or removed it by hand: an allowed device signs itself up again after your next sign-in, a removed one does not, and on a shared device nobody receives someone else's notifications unasked. It is deleted six months after the device was last signed up, once the push service no longer knows the address, or when you delete your account, whichever comes first.
- Notifications are delivered through the push service of the browser's maker, depending on the browser Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft. The content is end-to-end encrypted; the push service only sees the delivery address, the time and the size.
- Your device shows the full text of the notification, and for chat messages the conversation, who writes and what, also on the lock screen, unless you turned that off in the device's settings.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time with effect for the future.
