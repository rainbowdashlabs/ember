## Sending email

This instance sends its email through Sweego. This concerns only the messages the application itself triggers: invitations, notifications, confirmations and messages about signing in.

**Processor:**

**Server location:** European Union

**Privacy policy of the provider:** https://www.sweego.io/privacy-policy

### What is passed on

Sweego receives:

- your email address
- your name, where it appears in the salutation
- subject and content of the message in question
- the time of sending and technical delivery information

Sweego reports back to us whether a message was delivered or refused. That report contains your email address and the reason for a refusal.

### Legal basis and objection

The transfer rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Sweego.

You can switch notifications off at any time under Account → Notifications. Messages belonging to signing in or to the security of your account are sent regardless, for as long as your account exists.

### Retention at the provider

Delivery logs are kept by Sweego for {{ mailversand.aufbewahrung }} and deleted afterwards. Contents are not stored beyond that.
