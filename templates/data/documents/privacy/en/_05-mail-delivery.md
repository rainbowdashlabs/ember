## Sending email

This instance sends its email through a service provider. This concerns only the messages the application itself triggers: invitations, notifications, confirmations and messages about signing in.

**Processor:**
{{ mailversand.anbieter }}
{{ mailversand.anschrift }}

**Server location:** {{ mailversand.standort }}

**Privacy policy of the provider:** {{ mailversand.datenschutz }}

### What is passed on

The provider receives:

- your email address
- your name, where it appears in the salutation
- subject and content of the message in question
- the time of sending and technical delivery information

### Legal basis and objection

The transfer rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with the provider.

You can switch notifications off at any time under Account → Notifications. Messages belonging to signing in or to the security of your account are sent regardless, for as long as your account exists.

### Retention at the provider

Delivery logs are kept by the provider for {{ mailversand.aufbewahrung }} and deleted afterwards. Contents are not stored beyond that.
