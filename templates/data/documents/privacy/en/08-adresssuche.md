## Address search

Anyone looking up the station's address or a place of an appointment on the map can use the address search. It is only on when the operator of this instance chose it in the settings.

- The search uses Photon. Unless the operator runs their own server, the request goes to the public server of komoot (photon.komoot.io).
- Only the searched text is sent, that is the address or the place text of the appointment, along with the station's country and location as a hint for the search. The request is made by the server of this instance, not by your browser; your IP address and your account are not sent.
- Place texts are sent as they were entered. A place such as "at the Miller family, 5 Main Street" contains personal data; such places should be pinned on the map by hand without the address search.
- Answers are kept on the server for up to a day, so the same search is not asked again.

The legal basis is the legitimate interest in showing the places of appointments and the station's location on a map (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
