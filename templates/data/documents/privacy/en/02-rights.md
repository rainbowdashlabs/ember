## Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of access** (Art. 15 GDPR): You can download a copy of all your stored data at any time (under Settings → Data Export). The export contains all your data in JSON format.
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR): You can edit your profile data at any time.
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR): You can delete your account at any time. All personal data will be anonymised or removed. Your email address will be deleted and your name will be anonymised.
- **Right to restriction** (Art. 18 GDPR): Please contact your station manager.
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR): Data export is provided in machine-readable JSON format.
- **Right to object** (Art. 21 GDPR): You can object to processing at any time by deleting your account.
- **Right to withdraw consent**: You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.
