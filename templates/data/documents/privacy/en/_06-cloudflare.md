## Access through Cloudflare

This instance is reached through a Cloudflare tunnel. That means every request to this application passes Cloudflare's servers before it reaches us. Cloudflare shields the entrance against overload attacks and establishes the encrypted connection.

**Processor:**
Cloudflare, Ltd.
County Hall / The Riverside Building, Belvedere Road, London SE1 7PB, United Kingdom

Technical processing additionally takes place at the parent company, Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, United States of America.

**Privacy policy of the provider:** https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

### What is processed

Because all traffic passes through Cloudflare, the following is processed there:

- your IP address
- the date and time of the request
- the address requested within the application
- the amount of data transferred and technical details of the connection
- your browser identification and, where sent, the page you came from

The connection is decrypted at Cloudflare and encrypted again on its way to us. Cloudflare is therefore technically able to see the content passing through, but processes it solely on our instructions.

### Legal basis

The processing rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest lies in running the application securely and without interruption. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Cloudflare.

### Transfers to third countries

The United Kingdom is recognised by an adequacy decision of the European Commission as offering an adequate level of protection. Where data is processed in the United States beyond that, the transfer rests on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR.

Despite those arrangements, access by United States authorities cannot be ruled out entirely. Reaching this application without Cloudflare is not technically provided for.

### Retention

Cloudflare keeps the connection data named above for {{ cloudflare.aufbewahrung }} and deletes it afterwards.
