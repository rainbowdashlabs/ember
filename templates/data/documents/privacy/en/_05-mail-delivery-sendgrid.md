## Sending email

This instance sends its email through Twilio SendGrid. This concerns only the messages the application itself triggers: invitations, notifications, confirmations and messages about signing in.

**Processor:**
Twilio Inc.
101 Spear Street, First Floor, San Francisco, CA 94105, United States of America

**Server location:** United States of America

**Privacy policy of the provider:** https://www.twilio.com/en-us/legal/privacy

### What is passed on

Twilio SendGrid receives:

- your email address
- your name, where it appears in the salutation
- subject and content of the message in question
- the time of sending and technical delivery information

Twilio SendGrid reports back to us whether a message was delivered or refused. That report contains your email address and the reason for a refusal.

### Transfer to a third country

Your data is transferred to the United States, that is to a country outside the European Union. The transfer rests on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses under Art. 46(2)(c) GDPR, which form part of the data processing agreement.

Despite those arrangements, access by United States authorities cannot be ruled out entirely. If you would rather avoid it, please do without email notifications; the settings for that are under Account → Notifications.

### Legal basis and objection

The transfer rests on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with Twilio.

You can switch notifications off at any time under Account → Notifications. Messages belonging to signing in or to the security of your account are sent regardless, for as long as your account exists.

### Retention at the provider

Delivery logs are kept by Twilio SendGrid for {{ mailversand.aufbewahrung }} and deleted afterwards. Contents are not stored beyond that.
