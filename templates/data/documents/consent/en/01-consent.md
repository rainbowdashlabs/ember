# Consent to Data Processing

To use this application, your consent to the processing of your personal data and agreement to the terms of service is required.

## What We Store

- **Account data**: Email address, first and last name
- **Session data**: Browser information and approximate location (country) for account security
- **Profile and usage data**: Attendance, events, inventory and other data managed by your station

What of this is kept in your browser is listed in full in the "Storage in your browser" section.

## How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for the organisation and management of your youth group. **We do not share any personal data with third parties.**

## Your Rights

You can at any time:
- View and export your data (Settings → Data Export)
- Edit your profile
- Permanently delete your account and anonymise all data
- Withdraw this consent

By agreeing, you confirm that you have read the privacy policy and terms of service and consent to the processing of your data for the stated purposes.
