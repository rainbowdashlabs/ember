## Section 17 Admitting a station

(1) Where the operator offers this, youth groups can apply for their own station on this Platform. The application is made through the form provided and confirmed through a link sent by email.

(2) The operator decides on admission at its own discretion. There is no entitlement to admission, and a rejection needs no reasons.

(3) The applicant warrants that they are authorised to represent the youth group.

(4) The operator may make admission conditional on concluding additional agreements, in particular a data processing agreement.

## Section 18 Obligations of the station

The station is responsible towards the operator for ensuring

- that its members and their legal guardians are informed about the use of the Platform and that the necessary consents exist,
- that it maintains its own privacy policy where it is obliged to do so,
- that the profile fields, forms and registration questions it sets up collect only information necessary for its purposes,
- that access rights within the station are granted only to the extent required and are withdrawn when people leave,
- that the content it publishes is lawful, including the information legally required for a public homepage,
- that its members comply with these terms.

## Section 19 Data protection roles

(1) For the personal data processed in its station, the station is the controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation, unless otherwise agreed in an individual case. The operator processes that data on the station's behalf.

(2) Where the operator runs the Platform for a station of its own, it is the controller for that station's data itself.

(3) The details of processing on behalf, including further service providers used and the technical and organisational measures, are governed by a separate agreement between operator and station. In relation to members, the privacy policy of this Platform applies in addition.

(4) Requests from data subjects are addressed to the station. The operator supports the station using the functions available in the Platform.

## Section 20 Storage space and fair use

(1) The operator may set a storage allowance per station as well as limits on the size of individual files and on the number of emails sent. The applicable values can be viewed in the Platform.

(2) Where an allowance is exhausted, further uploads may be refused. Before imposing a restriction going beyond refusing individual uploads, the operator will inform the station and give it an opportunity to remove content.

(3) The operator may adjust allowances. A reduction will be announced to the station with reasonable notice.

## Section 21 Ending a station's use

(1) The station may end its use at any time without notice.

(2) The operator may end the use with three months' notice to the end of a month. The right to terminate for good cause remains unaffected; good cause exists in particular in the case of serious or repeated breaches of Sections 9, 12 or 18.

(3) Before use ends, the station is given the opportunity to export its data. After a reasonable period notified by the operator, the station's data is deleted or anonymised. Existing partnerships with other instances come to an end.
