## Section 5 Access and account

(1) Using the protected areas requires an account. Accounts are created by invitation from a station, by transfer from a waiting list or, where enabled, by self-registration.

(2) The information given when signing up must be truthful and complete and must be kept up to date. An email address may be required to confirm access; certain functions require a confirmed address.

(3) An account is personal. It may only be used by the person it was created for. Passing access credentials to third parties is not permitted.

(4) There is no entitlement to an account. Admission to a station is decided by its management.

## Section 6 Minors and legal guardians

(1) By its purpose, the Platform is also aimed at minors.

(2) For persons under the age of 16, use is only permitted with the consent of the holder of parental responsibility. The station ensures that this consent exists before an account is created.

(3) Legal guardians can be set up with their own account as manager of their child's account. They can view and edit the child's profile data, respond to events on the child's behalf and receive notifications. They are answerable for the child's use.

(4) Declarations concerning these terms - in particular agreement to an amended version - are made by the legal guardian on behalf of the child where the child cannot validly act itself.

## Section 7 Account security

(1) Access credentials must be kept secret and protected from third parties. Any suspicion of unauthorised use must be reported to the station management or the operator without delay.

(2) The Platform provides security functions which the operator or the station may make mandatory, in particular two-factor authentication, an overview of active sessions with the option to end individual sessions, and a check of the chosen password against publicly known data breaches. Where such a measure is made mandatory, access is restricted until it has been set up.

(3) Sessions expire after a period of inactivity set by the operator. Tokens for invitations, email confirmation and password resets are time-limited.

(4) Personal access tokens, in particular those for calendar, RSS and Atom feeds, are access credentials within the meaning of this section. They must not be published or passed on to third parties.

(5) Users are responsible for damage arising from a breach of these obligations.
