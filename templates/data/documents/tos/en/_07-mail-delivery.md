## Section 30 Email delivery through a service provider

(1) The operator sends the email of this platform through {{ mailversand.anbieter }}. This concerns the messages the platform itself triggers, in particular invitations, notifications and messages about signing in.

(2) Users keep a working email address for as long as their account exists. A message counts as received once it has been sent to the stored address and no delivery failure has been reported.

(3) The operator owes no particular delivery time and no delivery to addresses that refuse it, filter the sender out or cannot be reached. Addresses that repeatedly fail may be excluded from sending until the user corrects them.

(4) Notifications can be switched off in the account settings. Messages belonging to signing in or to the security of the account are not affected.

(5) The privacy policy of this platform governs the processing carried out by the provider.
