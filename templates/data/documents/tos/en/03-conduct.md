## Section 8 Permitted use

(1) The Platform may only be used for organising and managing the respective youth group and for purposes directly connected with that.

(2) Users may only access areas assigned to them, and may use data they learn in the course of their role - in particular contact details, emergency contacts, attendance records, test results and notes on other members - only for that purpose and must not disclose it outside the station.

(3) Exports and printouts from the Platform regularly contain personal data. They must be treated accordingly, kept securely and destroyed once the purpose no longer applies.

## Section 9 Prohibited use

The following are prohibited in particular:

- use for unlawful purposes or in breach of the rights of others
- posting content that is insulting, defamatory, discriminatory, harmful to minors, glorifying violence or otherwise unlawful
- posting content for which the necessary rights are missing, in particular images of other people without their consent
- introducing malware or files that endanger the Platform or its users
- circumventing or probing access restrictions, and exploiting security vulnerabilities beyond what is needed to report them
- automated access to the Platform without the operator's agreement, other than retrieving the provided feeds as intended
- any action impairing the availability of the Platform, in particular excessive requests, bulk uploads or triggering mass emails
- using the Platform or the data accessible through it for advertising, for disclosure to third parties or for any other purpose foreign to the Platform

## Section 10 Content and rights

(1) Whoever posts content is responsible for it. A station is responsible for its own content, in particular its public homepage, published news and publicly released articles.

(2) Anyone posting content warrants that they hold the necessary rights.

(3) By posting content you grant the operator the non-exclusive right, unrestricted as to territory and limited to the duration of storage, to store the content, process it technically, reproduce it and make it available to authorised recipients as part of operating the Platform. Technical processing includes in particular converting image and document formats, creating previews and producing exports. No right of use for other purposes is granted.

(4) Where a station releases content to partner stations or to the public, the right under paragraph 3 extends to the transmission and display required for that. Section 13 remains unaffected.

(5) The station management and the operator may remove content or make it inaccessible where it breaches these terms or applicable law.

(6) For operational reasons, certain functions keep change histories, for example on profile fields, equipment movements, checklists, notes and ticket activity. Those histories are retained after the original content is changed or deleted, to the extent required for traceability. Deleted comments may be retained as an empty placeholder so that reply chains remain readable.

## Section 11 Reporting violations and security vulnerabilities

(1) Unlawful content and breaches of these terms can be reported to the station management or the operator. The operator reviews reports received and decides on measures under Section 21.

(2) Security vulnerabilities must be reported to the operator only and kept confidential until they are fixed. They may be investigated only as far as is necessary for the report; accessing other people's data in the process is not permitted.
