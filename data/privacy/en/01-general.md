# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Controller

The data controller for data processing on this platform is the operator of the respective station. Contact details can be found in your station settings.

## 2. Legal Basis

The processing of personal data is based on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest). The legitimate interest lies in the organisation and management of the youth group. The fundamental rights and freedoms of data subjects, especially children, are always taken into account.

## 3. Data Collected

The following personal data is collected and processed:

### Account Data
- Email address, first and last name
- Password (stored only as a cryptographic hash)
- Profile picture (optional)

### Session Data
- Browser information (user agent)
- IP-based location (country code via Cloudflare header)
- Login time and last activity timestamp
- Session token

### Profile Data
- Station-defined profile fields (e.g. date of birth, address, phone number)
- Group memberships and tags
- Role assignments (e.g. member, team, manager)

### Usage Data
- Attendance records (status, check-in/check-out times)
- Event registrations and cancellations
- Inventory assignments and history (helmets, boots, clothing, etc.)
- Exchange and procurement requests
- Form responses (surveys, questionnaires)

### Communication
- News posts and comments
- Notification preferences and history

### Consent Records
- Time of consent, IP address, location (country), browser
- Version numbers of documents valid at the time of consent

## 4. Purpose of Processing

Data is processed exclusively for the following purposes:

- Organisation of the youth group (attendance, events, inventory)
- Member management and communication
- Notifications about relevant activities
- Proof of consent in accordance with GDPR
- Account security (session management, location display)

## 5. Data Sharing

**We do not share any personal data with third parties.** All data remains within the platform and is processed exclusively by the respective station team.

If email notifications are enabled, your email address is transmitted to the configured email service provider. Provider details can be found in the settings.

## 6. Retention Period

- **Active accounts**: Data is stored as long as the account is active.
- **Former members**: When a member is archived, profile data is retained or removed depending on configuration.
- **Deleted accounts**: Upon account deletion, all personal data is anonymised or removed.
- **Sessions**: Expired sessions are automatically deleted.
