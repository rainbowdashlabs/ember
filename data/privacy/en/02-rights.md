## 7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of access** (Art. 15 GDPR): You can download a copy of all your stored data at any time (under Settings → Data Export). The export contains all your data in JSON format.
- **Right to rectification** (Art. 16 GDPR): You can edit your profile data at any time.
- **Right to erasure** (Art. 17 GDPR): You can delete your account at any time. All personal data will be anonymised or removed. Your email address will be deleted and your name will be anonymised.
- **Right to restriction** (Art. 18 GDPR): Please contact your station manager.
- **Right to data portability** (Art. 20 GDPR): Data export is provided in machine-readable JSON format.
- **Right to object** (Art. 21 GDPR): You can object to processing at any time by deleting your account.
- **Right to withdraw consent**: You may withdraw your consent at any time. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

## 8. Browser Storage

This application uses your browser's **local storage** to store:

- Your session token (for authentication)
- Your station selection
- Your consent decision and version
- Your preferred theme (light/dark)

**No tracking cookies** or advertising cookies are used. No third-party tracking takes place.

## 9. Data Security

- Data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS.
- Passwords are stored exclusively as cryptographic hashes (bcrypt).
- Session tokens are cryptographically generated and have a limited validity period.
- Data access is restricted by role-based permissions.

## 10. Minors

This platform is used in particular for the management of youth groups. For children under 16, consent must be given or approved by the holder of parental responsibility. Parents can manage their children's profiles as member managers.

Changes to this privacy policy are versioned. In the event of material changes, you will be asked to consent to the new version.
